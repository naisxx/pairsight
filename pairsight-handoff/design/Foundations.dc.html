Design system · 01 · Foundations

One tile,
one pair.

PairSight lays a vendor's answers over an independent checker's and shows where they agree. The identity is built from that: every checked pair is a tile, and its colour says who called it the same. Charcoal and paper carry the page; vermilion and ice are the only two colours with a job.

The idea, in four marks

The mosaic

One tile per checked pair, 22 across. Ink: both say same. Vermilion: only the vendor. Ice: only the checker. Rule grey: both say different. It is the hero, the footer mark and a true chart.

The mark

Three tiles in a row: vendor, agreement, checker. The middle tile takes the text colour, so it is ink on paper and paper on charcoal. Minimum height 16px.

DifferentDifferent

The doubled word

One word in the hero is set twice: solid vermilion and an ice outline that stops slightly out of register. Used once per page.

=≠

Same and different

Equals and not-equals tiles label sections and rows. Ice equals for things that hold, vermilion not-equals for caveats and disagreements.

Colour

Dark is the default theme on the landing page; light is the default inside the product. Every text pairing is 4.5 to 1 or better. Vermilion fills take near-black text; on paper, text and links use the darker vermilion.

Shape, space and ground

Corners

Nothing is a circle except knobs and dots. 4 for mosaic tiles, 8 for glyph tiles and tags, 12 to 14 for buttons and inputs, 20 to 28 for cards and the hero panel.

The two-part button

A label and an end-cap divided by a seam, 14px corners, 54px tall on the landing page and 44px in the product. Secondary buttons are a 1px outline with no cap.

Spacing

A 4px grid. Landing sections sit 136px apart, 88px on a phone. Content is 1,216px wide with 32px gutters. Bento cards have 16px gaps and 28px padding.

The ground

A 48px hairline grid with a dot at each crossing, barely above the page colour. It is absent from the header, the pair ticker and the footer. See Motion for how it lights.
