≠What it measures

A second opinion.
Not a certificate.

≠Agreement is not proof.

The checker can be wrong. Open any pair and use your own judgement.

≠Unchecked pairs are not counted.

Pairs nobody suggested and no vendor matched are never checked.

≠A higher score is not a winner.

One vendor may merge more carefully while another finds more duplicates.

Does PairSight do the matching?=

No. Your vendors do the matching. PairSight checks their results against the checker's answers on the same pairs.

Who is Jev?=

Jev is the AI checker. It reads the two records and your definition of "the same", and gives a score between 0 and 1.

Is Jev always right?=

No. Its answers show where to look, not who is right.
