Design system · 03 · Motion

Light follows
attention.

Three things move on the landing page: the ground lights where the pointer is, the mosaic recolours when the vendor changes, and the how-it-works story plays as you scroll. Everything else is a small arrival or a hover. Inside the product nothing loops and nothing runs longer than 240ms.

Notes for the build

Scroll-linked effects need a fallback

The design uses CSS scroll timelines, which today run in Chrome and Edge only. Every element's resting style is its final, visible state, so other browsers show the three how-it-works steps stacked and static. For Safari and Firefox, drive the same keyframes from scroll position in script.

The grid glow is two CSS variables

A single pointer listener on the document writes the pointer's x and y, relative to the page, onto the grid layer. The masks read them. Do not route it through component state: it must not re-render the page.

Reduced motion

When the system asks for less motion, all animation and transitions are off and the page shows its settled state. The grid stays, with its glow at the rest position.

Small screens

Below 900px the how-it-works section does not pin: the three steps stack. The floating chips are hidden and the bento becomes one column.
