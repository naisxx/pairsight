≠What it measures

A second opinion.
Not a certificate.

≠Agreement is not proof.

Vendors are compared with the checker's answers, and the checker can be wrong. Open any pair and use your own judgement.

≠Unchecked pairs are not counted.

Pairs nobody suggested and no vendor matched are never checked, so duplicates among them stay unseen.

≠A higher score is not a winner.

One vendor may merge more carefully while another finds more duplicates. You decide which matters more.

=Your data stays yours.

Only the values you tick are sent to the checker. Delete an evaluation and its records go with it.

Questions

Asked
and answered.

Does PairSight do the matching?=

No. Your vendors do the matching. PairSight checks their results against the checker's answers on the same pairs, and shows where they agree and where they do not.

Who is Jev?=

Jev is the AI checker. It reads the two records and your definition of "the same", and gives a score between 0 and 1.

Is Jev always right?=

No. Jev is an AI and can be wrong. Its answers show where to look, not who is right.

What do I need to start?=

Your records as a file, and the matches at least one vendor found in them.
